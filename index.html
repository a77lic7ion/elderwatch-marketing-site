What the staff do

Everything the morning needs, on one screen.

Register a resident in under a minute — a name, a room, a phone, an emergency contact and a care note. Or import the whole register at once.
Respond to an alert — red jumps to the top of the board with the room number, and the family contact is on the same card.
Mark someone away — hospital, holiday or family. Excluded from the morning until they're back.
Log the visit — "in-person visit", time-stamped, with the sister's name on it.
Set the cutoff — each home picks the time that suits its own rounds.
Hand over — the daily sheet, plus 7-day and 30-day reports for the manager or the board.

Group managers see the homes you assign to them. A home's staff see only their own residents.

Getting a home onto it

One afternoon, then it runs itself.

No hardware to buy. Nothing to install from an app store. The residents keep the phones they already have.

  1. Send us your register — a spreadsheet is fine.
  2. We pair the phones at morning tea, with the sisters there. One code per resident, typed once.
  3. You set the cutoff. We check in weekly for the first month.
  4. Your first daily sheet arrives the next morning.

Security

Your residents' data is secure, all the time.

Only your staff can see your residents

The system refuses anyone else — not just the screen. Group managers see only the homes you assign; a home's staff see only their own.

The phones hold nothing

A phone is bound to one resident and keeps no copy of anyone's details. Lost or stolen, there's nothing on it to read.

Locked in transit and on the record

Everything travels encrypted. Staff passwords are hashed and sign-ins are rate-limited. Residents never sign in at all.

We've attacked it ourselves, the way an outsider would: no resident names, no check-ins, no staff accounts — and nothing could be changed.
